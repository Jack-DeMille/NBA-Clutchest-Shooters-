Why it matters

Which players have the clutch gene? And who crumbles under the pressure?

Legacies are defined by clutch moments.

A shot that ties or takes the lead late decides outcomes. Knowing who converts them informs who gets the ball.

Men lie, women lie, numbers don’t.

Game winners get replayed, misses fade. A full-history count puts a denominator behind every reputation.

Limited shot quantity.

Only about one qualifying shot per game league-wide, so a single player’s total stays small. That is why the attempt minimum matters.

Method

How a clutch shot is defined

1 · Pull every game

Play-by-play for each regular-season and playoff game, via nba_api.

2 · Keep the qualifying shots

Field-goal attempts in the 4th quarter or OT with ≤24 seconds left, where the shot would tie the game or take the lead.

3 · Aggregate per player

Career makes / attempts, with a 150-attempt minimum to filter out small-sample noise.
